Since a summer we still remember

Why Retro Poshak?

Because some things deserve to be worn, not just remembered.

The Idea

Memories are the inspiration. Every Retro Poshak piece begins with a small, specific moment — a bicycle bell at 7am, a gola cart in May, a deck of cards on a terrace at dusk.

The Design

Retro references are transformed into modern apparel graphics. We sketch, we distort, we simplify — until a memory becomes a wearable mark. Oversized cuts, heavyweight cotton, DTF prints.

The Feeling

Every piece should feel personal, nostalgic and wearable. If someone asks "where is that from?", you should want to tell them the story.

Meet the Founder

Urvashi Naik

Retro Poshak was created to transform personal and cultural memories into contemporary clothing. Urvashi grew up surrounded by the small textures of Indian life — and wanted to bottle them, not on a shelf, but on a sleeve.

"We don't just sell clothes. We turn memories into something you can wear."
