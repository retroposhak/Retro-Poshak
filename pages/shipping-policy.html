Shipping Policy

Every order is packed with care and shipped across India. Here's everything you need to know about delivery.

Shipping Timelines

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. Once dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS.

  • Metro Cities: 2–4 business days
  • Other Cities: 4–6 business days
  • Remote / Rural Areas: 6–9 business days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to weather, festivals, or courier delays. We'll keep you updated at every step.

Shipping Charges

  • Free Shipping: On all orders above ₹1,499
  • Standard Shipping: ₹79 for orders below ₹1,499
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Available across India at no extra charge
Note: Shipping charges are calculated at checkout and displayed before payment.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking ID via email and SMS. You can use this ID to check the status of your delivery anytime.

You can also track your order directly from our website by visiting the Track Order page.

International Shipping

Currently, we ship only within India. We're working on bringing Retro Poshak to more countries soon — follow us on social media for updates.

Undelivered Packages

If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address, repeated failed delivery attempts, or refusal at the door, we'll contact you to arrange a re-shipment. Additional shipping charges may apply.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your order is lost in transit or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of the expected delivery date with your order ID and photos (if applicable). We'll resolve it as quickly as possible.

Our team is here to help with any shipping questions.
