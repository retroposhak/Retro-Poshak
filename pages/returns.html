Returns & Exchanges

Not the right fit? No worries. We offer easy returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery.

Return Window

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order. After this window, we're unable to accept returns.

Important: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

Eligibility for Return

To be eligible for a return or exchange, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and free from any stains or odours
  • Original tags and packaging intact
  • Return request raised within 7 days of delivery
  • Proof of purchase (order ID) available

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
  • Custom or personalised products
  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper washing

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log in to your account and go to My Orders.
  2. Select the item you'd like to return or exchange.
  3. Choose a reason and submit your request.
  4. Our team will approve the request within 24–48 hours.
  5. Schedule a pickup or ship the item back using the address we provide.

Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund or ship the exchange within 5–7 business days.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer — our team will contact you for details.

  • Prepaid Orders: Refund to source in 5–7 business days
  • COD Orders: Refund via bank transfer in 7–10 business days

Exchanges

Want a different size or colour? We offer free exchanges within the 7-day window, subject to availability. If the requested size or colour is out of stock, we'll issue a full refund.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and photos. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no questions asked.
