Size Guide

Every body is different. Use our measurements to find the fit that feels like it was made for you.

How to Measure

For the most accurate fit, measure yourself over a light t-shirt. Keep the tape snug but not tight. If you're between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed, oversized fit.

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level under your arms and across your shoulder blades.

Length

Measure from the highest point of your shoulder (near the neck) straight down to where you want the hem to sit.

Sleeve

Measure from the shoulder seam down to the end of the sleeve, with your arm slightly bent.

Fit Guide

Oversized Fit

Relaxed through the body with dropped shoulders and a longer hem. This is our signature silhouette — made to feel lived-in from day one.

Regular Fit

Classic straight cut, true to size. If you prefer a closer fit to the body, choose your regular size.

Slim Fit

Tailored closer to the body with a narrower chest and sleeve. Slightly tapered through the waist.
